## 📈 **GROWTH TRIGGERS**

**When to Upgrade Legal Compliance:**

**First $1,000 MRR:**
- Register business entity
- Get business insurance

**100+ Users:**
- Professional legal review
- Formal privacy policy update

**1,000+ Users or EU Users:**
- DPO service
- Vendor agreements
- Full compliance review

**$10,000+ MRR:**
- Everything in the full checklist
- Regular legal audits

## 🚨 **RED FLAGS - Stop and Get Legal Help:**

- Complaint from data protection authority
- User threatens legal action over privacy
- Significant data breach or security incident
- Venture capital interest (they'll require compliance)
- Enterprise customers asking for compliance docs

## 💡 **STARTUP-FRIENDLY TIPS**

1. **Use your existing tech setup** - your privacy features are already better than 90% of MVPs
2. **Personal liability is OK temporarily** - most founders start this way
3. **EU users increase risk** - consider geo-blocking EU initially if concerned
4. **Document everything** - save all your compliance decisions for later legal review
5. **Be transparent** - users appreciate honesty about beta status
6. **Keep it simple** - removed unnecessary age verification for B2B tool
